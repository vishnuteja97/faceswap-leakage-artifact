Camera-ready artifact audit — 2026-09-30
Reference: paper/camera_ready.tex (SHA-256 in data/camera_ready_reference.json)

Release scope
-------------
This is a local update of the artifact. Source manuscripts and experiment outputs
were not edited. No new face swaps were generated. Saved scores were exported,
and missing cascade negative scores were recovered from retained embeddings on
CPU. Face/context composites and recognition were rerun on retained images to
recover complete negative scores. Linear operators were refitted on CPU from
exported aggregate statistics. The figure-source Monte Carlo calculation was
replayed on CPU. These recovery operations are distinguished from historical runs.

Confirmed numerical coverage
----------------------------
* All 84 single-swap table entries reproduce from similarity_scores.csv.
* All 75 cascade passes reproduce saved target medians, pooled-control floors,
  AUC, and quantile-threshold TPR at 1% and 0.1% nominal FPR.
* Deep-tail fits from passes 8..25 reproduce 0.966, 0.935, 0.946.
* Pass-25 AUCs reproduce 0.602, 0.599, 0.553.
* Operator rho(B) and sigma_max(B) reproduce directly from the saved B matrices.
* All 45 face/context table entries independently reproduce from complete scores:
  37140 target scores and 371400 negatives. Maximum target-score error against
  the original CSV is zero; maximum AUC error against saved results is 1.11e-16.
* Three A/B/c fits are independently solved from normal equations. Held-out R^2
  and b_u reproduce the manuscript values. Identity-disjoint train/test splits
  are checked from released donor/target identity manifests.
* Original single-swap scores, rankings, operators, and five-pass measurements
  remain preserved in legacy/original_submission/. The updated rankings add the
  missing pair_id join without changing any rank or gallery size.

Known manuscript discrepancies and distinctions
----------------------------------------------
1. Cohort: methods claim a common 947-pair panel, but the published table uses
   948 valid pairs for six tools and 947 for DiffFace. pair_0715 accounts for the
   difference. Default reproduction retains the table inputs and labels them.
   --common-cohort is a separately labelled sensitivity analysis.

2. Means: default artifact FaceFusion target mean is 0.09749953 (prints 0.097 at
   three decimals), versus paper 0.098; E4S donor mean is 0.24545114 versus paper
   0.246. The common 947-pair means are 0.09760583 and 0.24562222, respectively.
   These tiny differences should not be concealed by relabelling the cohorts.
   The unused/legacy ffDonorSim macro 0.453 also differs from mean 0.45246024.

3. First-pass FaceFusion excess ratio: the revised pooled floor and the figure's
   0.616 calibration give 0.138 (approximately 0.13804), whereas prose retains
   the original five-pass-analysis value 0.139. The artifact computes the revised
   ratio and preserves the old calculation only as historical evidence.

4. Thresholds: the deep evaluator's 99th/99.9th percentile negative threshold is
   not a strict empirical <=1%/<=0.1% FPR rule. With 4780 negatives, nominal 1%
   gives actual 48/4780 = 1.004184%; with 9990 negatives, 100/9990 = 1.001001%.
   The new output shows both conventions and achieved FPR rather than silently
   substituting one. Individual negatives within a chain are not independent
   people-level trials; this release does not provide inferential confidence
   intervals or new population-wide privacy guarantees.

5. Floors and prediction metrics must be distinguished. The saved auxiliary
   floor values in aux_predicted_floors.json are 0.004363889 (BlendFace)
   and 0.001853371 (CanonSwap), versus prose 0.008 and 0.003. The replayed current
   figure-source simulation gives final-three-pass mean unrelated cosines of
   0.004672916, 0.005518881, and -0.000665397 (FaceFusion, BlendFace, CanonSwap).
   Those Monte Carlo averages are not Lyapunov plateaus. The older FaceFusion
   predicted_curve.json is kept separately and is not substituted for the current
   three-panel figure calculation. Simulations score mean cosine to a source-image
   direction; measured curves use medians against different-image galleries.

6. The manuscript says excess leakage below 0.01 took >25 passes depending on
   the tool. CanonSwap's measured pass-25 excess is already about 0.0085; the other
   tools remain above 0.01 there. A fitted crossing (25.2 for CanonSwap, 31.1 for
   BlendFace, 38.8 for FaceFusion) is an extrapolation/curve fit, not a measured
   threshold crossing. The reproduction labels it accordingly.

7. Model baseline table: six raw-space baseline rows have supporting recorded
   summaries in model_validation.json. The kNN row's 0.442 cosine and 0.16 R^2
   trace to a historical normalized-space run evaluated on 2000 examples, not
   the full 10667-example raw-space test protocol described by the table caption.
   Its actual provenance is retained in recorded/knn_baseline.json. The learning
   curve is also a separate fixed split (10443 test examples), with up to 56825
   training examples; it is not the main 57048/10667 operator split.

8. Persistent-direction correlations: the figure-source code forms 40 real/imaginary
   columns from the top 20 eigenvectors and uses every QR column, despite linear
   dependence from complex-conjugate eigenvectors. QR can add arbitrary orthogonal
   directions outside the intended eigenspace. Replaying this calculation can
   change correlations across numerical libraries. The release retains scalar
   alignments from that historical QR convention and adds a corrected SVD
   rank-aware basis. reproduce_diagnostics.py labels both; neither is relabelled
   to force agreement with the prose's 0.21/0.22/0.21 and approximately -0.02.
   Corrected diagnostic values are an artifact correction, not claimed to be
   the exact camera-ready measurements. Original score data and B are unchanged.

9. Monotonicity and ordering: deep per-pass medians/excess ratios fluctuate.
   Neither strict stepwise decrease nor a universal cross-tool ordering should
   be inferred from the overall decay trend. Deep-tail fits do not follow the
   fitted spectral-radius ordering. The original artifact mixed FaceFusion's
   fifth-pass ratio with other tools' third-pass ratios; current output uses
   the same 8..25 fitting range for all tools.

10. Regularization selection: FaceFusion uses five identity-disjoint folds, seed 1,
    and mean fold MSE. Auxiliary tools use one seed-11 80/20 ROW holdout and
    highest mean validation cosine, not five identity-disjoint folds as the
    manuscript's general description suggests. The final train/test identity
    split is disjoint for all three tools; this does not make auxiliary inner
    validation identity-disjoint. Released configs preserve the actual methods.

11. Harder non-members: the exact historical selected identities were not found.
    The released reconstruction selects the highest source-image cosine over all
    stored photos among the saved maximum-MAAD-score tie group, independently
    for each target; negative identity reuse is allowed. The saved top-1000
    ranking truncates 84 of 948 maximum-score groups. The mean retained group
    size is about 377, compared with about 445 before truncation. Complete
    reconstruction assignments, candidate scores and resulting swap scores are
    released, but they must not be described as the historical paper experiment.

Results that are recorded rather than independently recomputed
-------------------------------------------------------------
* The historical harder-non-member control remains summary-only; the independently
  computable reconstruction is a separate experiment with the discrepancies above.
* Fit cosine is recomputed from per-sample dot/norm scalars for the recovered
  solution. These scalars cannot evaluate arbitrary new model predictions.
  Auxiliary CV selection similarly uses per-candidate cosine components rather
  than independently rebuilding all candidate predictions from embeddings.
* Residual diagnostics, model baselines and learning curves remain saved results.
  Per-triplet embeddings and all fitted baseline models are not distributed.
  Aggregate normal equations now support main-operator refitting, R^2 and b_u;
  they do not reconstruct the original images or supply every baseline input.
* Figure Monte Carlo curves are recorded outputs of a replay of the source script;
  the full donor bank, residual model inputs, and training corpus are not released.
* Nonlinear-improvement and some formal residual-distribution claims do not have
  a complete independently reproducible input bundle here. The artifact does not
  claim to validate those manuscript statements.
* Image quality illustrations, t-SNE, visual examples, and end-to-end generation
  are outside the released score-based reproduction. No identifiable source
  photographs or synthetic illustration files are needed or bundled.

Original versus revised controls
--------------------------------
The first-five-pass FaceFusion target scores are unchanged. Original single
negative assignment used the ordered 500-chain manifest; revised assignment
uses the 478 completed-chain manifest, changing 430 assignments. Revised primary
AUC then pools ten controls per chain. Thus original pass-5 AUC 0.70882425 and
revised pooled AUC 0.71632893 are different evaluations of the same target scores.

Provenance limits
-----------------
External data/model/setup links and expected filenames are listed in
EXTERNAL_RESOURCES.md. The benchmark requires published VGGFace2-HQ images;
raw VGGFace2 and an additional universal alignment pass are not equivalent inputs.
Publisher pages were reviewed, but large downloads and a clean installation were
not retested. FaceShifter's primary source link is maum-ai/faceshifter, as cited in
camera_ready.tex. That footnote states that the authors trained their own model
on CelebA-HQ. The remaining gap is access to the authors' evaluated weights and
their training/loading provenance, not a missing official pretrained-model link.
The inspected local checkout has a different remote (graydove/FaceShifter), and
the bundled inference adapter expects *_280000.pth files. Their relationship to
the cited training implementation has not been established by this audit.

Exact 25-pass scoring galleries and control-chain assignments are exported using
dataset-relative paths. Additional donor schedules incorporate 46 FaceFusion
substitutions from retained completion logs (none recorded for auxiliary tools).
The logs do not independently attest skipped outputs or unlogged earlier retries.
Current tool commits, local modifications and checkpoint hashes are exported;
these are not independently attested historical generation versions. Reference
orchestration source is bundled with only machine path prefixes replaced.
Main-fit train/test identity disjointness is verified from the released manifests.
No claim is made that this release audits dataset identity labels, every raw image,
or every historical generation parameter. Public dataset identifiers
are not real-name identity annotations. Source hashes identify the local inputs
used for this export, not a certification of their original acquisition.

Validation
----------
Run validate.py and python -m unittest test_metrics.py test_refit.py. SHA-256 checks protect the
frozen release files. Numerical checks include recomputation, source-summary
comparisons, and manuscript-value comparisons, with their different meanings
labelled in the output. Known discrepancies are documented here rather than
turned into fabricated matching results.
